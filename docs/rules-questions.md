1. Should hidden Capital placement be simultaneous, alternating, or secretly committed then revealed?
2. Does “within 1 square” include diagonals?
3. When inserting beneath a Structure, may the player choose any legal stack depth?
4. Does victory count Structures and Capitals only, or all Permanents including Lands?
5. If both players lose their last qualifying permanent simultaneously, is the result a draw?
6. What are base HP values for Lands, Structures and Capitals when not printed?
7. Are tokens removed at the start of their controller's turn or their creator's turn?
