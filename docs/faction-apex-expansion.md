# Faction apex expansion

Each faction receives ten late-game game-changer cards costing 5–10 GP:

- five cards in its primary type;
- three cards in its secondary type;
- one card in each other deck type.

Capitals remain pregame cards and are not included in deck-type allocation.

| Faction | Primary type | Secondary type | Strategic purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zeus | Spell | Character | Decisive storm reactions backed by elite mobile attackers |
| Poseidon | Land | Character | Extremely resilient territory supporting abyssal guardians |
| Hades | Spell | Character | Late-game removal and protection enabling underworld finishers |
| Ares | Character | Spell | Overwhelming combat bodies supported by explosive battle magic |
| Athena | Character | Structure | Formation leaders protected by fortress-grade defenses |
| Hephaestus | Structure | Land | The strongest industrial board foundation and forge infrastructure |

## Starter construction

Each faction now owns 35 unique cards:

- the previous 25-card faction pool;
- ten apex cards.

The faction starter contains one copy of all 35 cards plus a second copy of its five primary-type apex cards. This creates exactly 40 cards, showcases the faction identity, and keeps every card at one or two copies—well below the four-copy limit.

## Balance principles

- Apex costs are 5–10 GP and cannot dominate opening turns.
- Apex Characters remain within Attack ≤ cost + 1 and Defense ≤ cost + 2.
- Apex Lands use 14–19 HP.
- Apex Structures use 16–24 HP.
- Apex Spells use the existing typed reaction system and have stronger effect values appropriate to their cost.
- High-cost cards can clog early hands and reduce the deck's supply of inexpensive Permanents, Characters, or reactions.
- All cards remain prototype values pending AI simulations and human playtests.
